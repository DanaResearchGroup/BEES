# Verified tumor data cover few BEES parameters

Only a handful of numbers for healthy-versus-tumor enzyme and metabolite levels were verified, and they cover only some of what the two BEES models need. The best-supported items are: thymidylate synthase (TS) activity ratios of **1.6x (colorectal) to ~10x (head and neck)**; colorectal 5,10-methylene-THF of **880 pmol/g** in untreated tumor; the human hexokinase Ki for 2-deoxyglucose (**0.074-0.15 mM**); methotrexate (MTX) polyglutamate retention and DHFR dissociation kinetics in breast cancer cells; and plasma MTX pharmacokinetics. Nothing verified gives an absolute DHFR, SHMT, HK, PGI or PFK concentration in any human tissue or cell. The proteomic-ruler datasets (Wisniewski/Mann, Nagaraj/Geiger, CPTAC, PaxDb) that would supply copy numbers were blocked in the research session, as were the tables in Hirayama 2009 and Sullivan 2019 that would supply glucose, G6P, F6P, FBP and ATP/ADP. The enzyme concentrations in `methotrexate_dhfr` and `glycolysis_2dg` therefore remain placeholders. A literature-grounded replacement exists for only a few parameters: the 2-DG Ki (placeholder 0.3 mM versus 0.074-0.15 mM), the MTX Ki and dosing timescales, and a tumor-versus-normal TS multiplier. Everything else here is an assumption-based conversion or a search-summary figure, labelled as such. Absence of a number here means "not retrieved", not "does not exist".

Verification labels used throughout. **V** means the number appeared in an abstract or full text the researchers read through the PubMed tool. **S** means it came only from a search-result snippet or a vendor/aggregator page, so attribution is uncertain. **A** means it is my arithmetic on assumed constants (about 200 mg/mL cell protein, about 2 pL cell volume, 0.7-0.8 water fraction, 1 g tissue = 1 mL), which is good to roughly 3-10x at best. Even V items were read at abstract level by the research team, not re-checked against raw data.

## Thymidylate synthase is the only folate-cycle enzyme with tumor-versus-normal numbers

TS has the best data of the folate enzymes, and the tumor excess is modest in the gut and large in head and neck. In 43 gastrointestinal patients, median TS activity was **49.8 versus 30.8 pmol/mg protein** in colorectal tumor versus normal mucosa (ratio 1.62) and 81.0 versus 38.0 in stomach (ratio 2.13); TS mRNA ran 3.0-3.5x higher in tumor ([Miyamoto 2001](https://doi.org/10.3892/ijo.18.4.705)). Protein, mRNA and activity did not correlate in that cohort. In 37 head-and-neck carcinomas, TS activity was **9.2 +/- 21.5 versus 0.9 +/- 1.2 pmol/min/mg** in adjacent mucosa, a ratio of means near 10x that the huge standard deviation makes unreliable ([Parise 1994](https://doi.org/10.1002/hed.2880160210)). In 13 colorectal patients, FdUMP-binding TS was **6.6 pmol/g in primary tumor, 3.2 in liver metastasis and 2.5 in normal mucosa** ([Umekita 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11086436/)), and gastric tumors spanned 7.1-176.6 fmol/mg protein ([Ishikawa 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10697632/)). A pediatric biopsy series reporting up to ~30x tumor excess is S only ([Peters 1988 summary](https://www.nature.com/articles/pr1988508)).

These assays do not agree in absolute terms. Miyamoto's pmol/mg values sit about 1000-fold above FdUMP-binding fmol/mg values, which suggests different assay conditions or a unit error, so the studies cannot be pooled. Converting the FdUMP-binding values gives TS in the **low nM range (about 1.4-35 nM, i.e. 1.4e-6 to 3.5e-5 mM)** (A). That is 15-350 times below the model's 5e-4 mM. Because TS is a dimer with one FdUMP site per subunit, site counts may overstate enzyme concentration by up to 2x.

DHFR and SHMT are the weak spots. Human DHFR is described only qualitatively as higher in GI, kidney, lung and breast cancers than in normal tissue, and the two MTX-titration values in that source differ about 2000-fold, so they are unusable ([Chulalongkorn record](https://cuir.car.chula.ac.th/handle/123456789/2154?mode=full)). SHMT2 mRNA is elevated in more than 85% of cancer types in TCGA, and protein is higher in lung adenocarcinoma in CPTAC, but no fold values were read ([PMC8408114](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8408114/); [PMC8049788](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8049788)). The only quantitative DHFR scaling is for resistant cells: CCRF-CEM sublines carry **10-20x DHFR activity with ~18 gene copies** and ~75-fold MTX resistance ([Rutgers record](https://www.researchwithrutgers.org/en/publications/molecular-and-karyological-analysis-of-methotrexate-resistant-and/); [Cellosaurus CLPUB00028](https://cellosaurus.org/cellopub/CLPUB00028)), and HCT-8R4 colon cells show 25x ([PMID 2914962](https://pubannotation.org/docs/sourcedb/PubMed/sourceid/2914962)). Extreme 160-300x amplification is mouse data.

Folate and nucleotide metabolites are thinner still. In 76 colorectal patients, LC-MS/MS gave untreated-tumor 5,10-methylene-THF of **880 pmol/g** (highest untreated value 1,714 pmol/g). In untreated controls, methylene-THF and THF were significantly higher in tumor than in mucosa, while 5-methyl-THF was higher in mucosa ([Taflin 2014](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00280-014-2591-9)). Per tissue water this is about 1.1-1.3 uM (A). Head-and-neck folate pools were similar between tumor and mucosa, 1.2 versus 0.8 pmol/mg protein (not significant; [Parise 1994](https://doi.org/10.1002/hed.2880160210)). Free NADPH measured with sensors in HeLa cells was **3.1 +/- 0.3 uM in cytosol and ~37 uM in mitochondria** (S; [Tao 2017](https://www.nature.com/articles/nmeth.4306.pdf)). Baseline dUMP of 2-5 pmol/10^6 cells, about 1-2.5 uM at 2 pL (A), is also S ([PMC348628](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC348628)). Serine, glycine, NADP+ and the NADPH/NADP+ ratio were not obtained. Park 2016 contains absolute iBMK (mouse) values ([Park 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/nchembio.2077)) but is not human and its supplementary tables were unreadable.

## Glycolysis: tumor hexokinase has higher glucose affinity, and tissue glucose is low

For upper glycolysis the verified information is about kinetics and direction, not concentration. Human tumor hexokinase isoenzymes have a glucose Km near **10^-6 M (0.001 mM)** versus ~10^-5 M (0.01 mM) in normal counterparts, because the low-affinity isoenzyme is lost ([Monakhov 1978](https://doi.org/10.1093/jnci/61.1.27)). Total HK activity is higher in tumors in most tissues but lower in glioma, 2-4x below normal rat brain in xenografts, with only 9-69% mitochondria-bound ([Oudard 1997](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9216643/)), because the comparator tissue is HK-rich. HT29 colon cells with the glycolytic phenotype had about 2x the HK, PFK-1 and PK activity of differentiated cells ([Gauthier 1989](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0005-2728(89)80253-1)). A high-Km (8-11.8 mM) glucose-phosphorylating activity of **1.1-20 nmol/min/mg protein** appeared in human tumor lines but not in untransformed cells ([Board 1995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7614462/)). This is a glucokinase-like fraction, not total HK. HK is consistently weaker than PFK in tumor homogenates, so the two can share flux control ([Board 1990](https://doi.org/10.1042/bj2650503)). PFKP is higher in most tumor types by TCGA and in CPTAC lung adenocarcinoma, without fold values ([Sci Rep 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-43982-2.pdf)).

Absolute concentrations are almost entirely missing. Hirayama's CE-TOFMS study of 16 colon and 12 stomach cancers reported "extremely low glucose" and high lactate and glycolytic intermediates in tumor, but the abstract gives no numbers ([PubMed 19458066](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19458066/)). Sullivan 2019 found tumor interstitial glucose below plasma in some mouse tumors and skin interstitial glucose similar to plasma, with values only in figures ([PMC6510537](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6510537)). No verified G6P, F6P, FBP, ATP or ADP value exists for normal or tumor tissue in these notes. Enzyme abundance is equally unresolved. The only anchors are a proteome-wide HeLa median of ~18,000 copies/cell (S; [BioNumbers 108425](https://bionumbers.hms.harvard.edu/bionumber.aspx?s=n&v=0&id=108425)), a PFK estimate of ~5e4 molecules/cell with no organism given (S; [Springer review](https://springerlink.bibliotecabuap.elogim.com/content/pdf/10.1007/BF01648926.pdf)), and the observation that glycolytic enzymes are the main cases where enzyme exceeds substrate ([Albe 1990](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0022-5193(05)80266-8)). At 2 pL, 5e4 copies is ~4e-5 mM (A). Dividing the Board 1995 activities by a rabbit-muscle HK kcat of ~200/s ([BioNumbers 100963](https://bionumbers.hms.harvard.edu/bionumber.aspx?id=100963)) gives 1-20 nmol/min/mg as ~1e-5 to 2.5e-4 mM (A). This brackets the model's HK value but rests on a non-human kcat.

## Methotrexate pharmacology is the best-covered area, and it supports the dosing design

The MTX notes supply real numbers for exposure, titration and retention. In L1210 cells, deoxyuridine incorporation fell below 2% of control above 1 uM extracellular MTX, and DHFR activity was undetectable above 0.5 uM. About 5% residual DHFR still sustained 50% of DNA synthesis, so roughly 95% inhibition is needed to halve it ([Bender & Makula 1978](https://doi.org/10.1038/bjc.1978.60)). Human DHFR binds MTX as a slow tight-binding inhibitor with **Ki = 3.4 pM** (V via a search summary of the paper's introduction; [Volpato 2009](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M109.018010)). This is a stoichiometric titration regime, so effect depends on total enzyme and uptake, not on Ki.

Polyglutamation sets duration. After 24 h at 2 uM, polyglutamates were **55.4% (51.9 nmol/g) of intracellular drug in MCF-7, 87.6% (62.4 nmol/g) in ZR-75-B and 32% (4.06 nmol/g) in MDA-231**, and sensitive lines still had 30-35% of control incorporation 24 h after washout ([Jolivet 1982](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci110624)). Dissociation half-lives from DHFR rise from **12 min (Glu1) to 30, 102, 108 and 120 min (Glu2-5)** ([Jolivet & Chabner 1983](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI111048)). Retention also varies by line: MCF-7 and ZR-75-B kept 22% and 51% of total drug after 24 h drug-free. As a rough check (A), a 12 min half-life means koff of 9.6e-4/s. Against Ki 3.4 pM that implies kon near 3e8 /M/s, close to the diffusion limit, whereas the ChEMBL-derived 0.1 nM implies a more plausible ~1e7 /M/s. This suggests the model's Ki is the better effective value for cellular conditions, though the 12 min measurement's temperature is not stated. Transport is known only in relative terms: RFC1 transfection raised influx 9-fold and steady-state free MTX only 2.1-fold ([Zhao 1997](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.272.34.21207)). No absolute Km or Vmax was retrieved.

Plasma kinetics bracket the model's elimination. After 12 g/m2 over 4 h in 34 patients, peak MTX was **674-1778 uM**, alpha half-life **2.42 +/- 0.45 h** and beta half-life **19.9 +/- 7.6 h** ([Raude 1988](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3209285/)). No cancer-versus-normal comparison of intracellular MTX threshold, tissue MTX or low-dose oral/IM plasma level was found. Liposome release data are S only: DPPC first-phase release of ~15-40 h ([Purdue dissertation](https://docs.lib.purdue.edu/dissertations/AAI8210268)). For 2-DG, a phase I trial chose 63 mg/kg/day, but no plasma concentration was available ([Raez 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23228990/)).

Two points deserve emphasis. First, the folate model's enzyme concentrations are not just placeholders but probably too high for TS by one to two orders of magnitude relative to tissue measurements. That changes the ratio of drug to enzyme that governs titration behaviour, so the release-profile conclusions should be re-run across a TS and DHFR range rather than a single value. Second, the 2-DG model has the same issue in glucose: with glucose Km probably 10-100x lower than the placeholder and tumor interstitial glucose reported as very low, competitive inhibition at Ki 0.074-0.15 mM may be weaker than the placeholder implies. Product inhibition by 2-DG-6-phosphate (Km 2 mM for the reverse reaction in rat brain; [PubMed 4004267](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4004267/)) is not captured.

## Missing data and the order to close it

The blocking factor was network access, so the missing items are mostly retrievable. Highest priority are the proteomic-ruler and Nagaraj/Geiger tables (copy numbers for DHFR, TYMS, SHMT1/2, HK1/2, GPI, PFKL/M/P), CPTAC or Human Protein Atlas fold changes, and the Park 2016 supplement for serine, glycine, NADP+, ATP, ADP, G6P, F6P and FBP in mammalian cells. Next are Hirayama 2009 and Sullivan 2019 data tables for tumor glucose and glycolytic intermediates, the Taflin 2014 tables for THF and 5-methyl-THF, and the Albe 1990 tables for enzyme site concentrations. Entirely absent from the retrieved literature are human DHFR/SHMT specific activities, any normal-versus-tumor comparison of intracellular MTX threshold, RFC Km and Vmax in absolute units, HK2-specific Km and 2-DG Ki, intracellular 2-DG-6-phosphate, plasma 2-DG Cmax, and a modern liposome release half-life in serum at 37 C.

## Conclusion

The sharpest consequence is that the strongest literature support sits on the drug side, not the enzyme side: MTX Ki, dissociation kinetics, plasma half-lives and the 2-DG Ki can be adopted with stated uncertainty, whereas every enzyme concentration in both models is unanchored by human data. The one exception is that TS tissue measurements imply nM concentrations, well below the model value. A defensible near-term step is to replace the 2-DG Ki and glucose Km, add a slow elimination phase to the MTX model, and run sensitivity sweeps over TS, DHFR, HK and PFK concentrations spanning two orders of magnitude, with the tumor-scenario multipliers (TS 1.6-10x, DHFR 10-25x in resistance, HK/PFK ~2x) applied as ratios.

The healthy-versus-tumor question also resists a single answer. TS excess ranges from 1.6x to ~10x, HK is lower in glioma and higher elsewhere, and folate pools show little difference in some cohorts. Tissue identity dominates, so a generic "tumor" parameter set would misrepresent most cancers. Parameterization should be per tissue, starting with colorectal, where TS activity, TS pmol/g, methylene-THF and glucose-depletion data all exist.
